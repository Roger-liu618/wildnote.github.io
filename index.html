About

A Voice for the Wild

Roger Liu

High School Student · Wildlife Enthusiast

I am a high school student with a deep love for wildlife. From watching documentaries about endangered species as a child to volunteering at local animal shelters today, the natural world has always been my deepest concern. Wildlife conservation, biodiversity, green development — these are not just academic terms, but the directions of my daily thought and action.

This website is my field journal. Here I record observations, organize thoughts, and share actions. If you too care about the future of wildlife on this planet, you are welcome to stay, read, and join the conversation.

Core Themes

Three Paths, One Wild

01

Wildlife Protection

Following the plight of endangered species and imagining coexistence between humans and wildlife. From snow leopards on the Tibetan Plateau to sea turtles in the Pacific, every species tells a story worth hearing.

02

Biodiversity

From a single wetland to an entire rainforest, understanding how the web of life is woven — and torn. Every species lost is a thread cut from the fabric of existence.

03

Green Development

Exploring how economic growth and ecological protection can cease to be enemies, and seeking paths to a sustainable future where both people and wildlife can thrive.
